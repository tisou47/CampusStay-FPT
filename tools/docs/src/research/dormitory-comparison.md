## B. Corrections to Duy's report

KTX side:
1. The BIDV guide is titled "Thanh toán bằng **Website** của KTX với ngân hàng BIDV"; "App" is not in it. Today's guides mention no official app.
2. The account 963111 + CCCD page does not say reconciliation is automatic; it tells the student to check the receipt status. Write it as "the receipt updates on the website", not "reconciled automatically by the bank".
3. "Closes after 7 days" means 7 days after each school's enrolment schedule ends (PLO), not 7 days after opening.
4. Article 17 has 3 disciplinary forms, but the appendix grades violations on 5 levels (reminder and criticism come first).
5. The iDorm app on Google Play (Binh Dinh Software, "independent project") could not be read at its source. *(unconfirmed: leave it out, or the team rechecks it on a phone.)*

CampusStay side (checked against the code):
6. "Giờ ra vào: Admin cấu hình" is wrong. CampusStay has no gate hours. Block settings hold visiting hours and quiet hours (`block.visit_*`, `block.quiet_from_latest`) within limits Admin sets.
7. "Xin lùi hạn: Không có" is wrong. When a late check-in is approved, the Balance deadline moves (`LateCheckInService` → `Billing.extendBalanceDeadline`).
8. "No early leaving" holds for an online request only. Staff can check a student out early at the desk, and the `EARLY_CHECK_OUT` rule refunds 30% of the unused days (configurable).
9. "Khách thăm: Chưa có" should read "only through Family Day".
10. Confirmed in the code: a 6-digit door code, with every viewing audited (`DOOR_CODE_VIEWED`); Android 8.0+ (minSdk 26); at most 9 Terms (`stay.max_terms`); Check value at 3× the previous use; a Utility termination 3 days after the final warning, triggered by 2 overdue months or a debt reaching the deposit; the Deposit kept (0%) when the Balance is unpaid; Family Day entry from 15 minutes before the slot, and guests under 18 accompanied.
